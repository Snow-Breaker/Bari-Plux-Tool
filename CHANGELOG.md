# Changelog

Confirmed public milestones for Bari Plux Tool. This is a summary, rather than a complete record of every patch. Current download availability and release details are maintained on the [official product page](https://bariplux.com/tool).

## Recent additions

- In-game performance overlay.
- Installed Files for emulators.

These additions were confirmed by the creator. Individual release dates and version numbers are not assigned here.

## 12 October 2026 — WinUI 3 rewrite released

- Official release of the rewritten PC application.
- Transition from the previous WPF implementation to WinUI 3.
- Broader application scope, including Game Mode, optimization, file management and the surrounding account and community experience.

[Read the rewrite case study](./docs/wpf-to-winui3.md).

## 16 November 2025 — First official PC release

- Official launch of Bari Plux Tool for PC.
- The project grew from personal graphics / FPS and GameLoop keymapping workflows, with later expansion shaped by community requests.
