# Roadmap

**Status reviewed: 21 October 2026.** These are product directions, not a release schedule. Priorities and scope may change as development progresses.

| Focus | Status | Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BPT for Windows** | Ongoing | Improve existing tools, performance, usability and compatibility, guided by reports and feedback. |
| **BPT Android** | Coming Soon | Develop gaming tools, device and network insight, file management, backups and shared account features. |
| **PC ↔ Android connection** | Planned | Bring both experiences together, including planned remote PC / GameLoop management. |
| **Beyond BPT** | Longer-term exploration | Explore new products and brands in other areas of software. |

Android capabilities will depend on device support, permissions and platform limitations. Some advanced features may require root. No Android release date has been announced.

Have a specific workflow in mind? See [how to suggest a feature](./SUPPORT.md#suggest-a-feature).
